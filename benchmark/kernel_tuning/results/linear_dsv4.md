# DeepSeek-V4-Flash Linear rows (SM5-DSV4)

Generated by `linear_dsv4_report.py` from the acceptance logs (20 pairs; GB10 60 pairs)
(`linear_smallm.py --accept --l2 cold|engine|hot`, shapes
`dsv4_bf16_shapes.json` / `dsv4_fp32_shapes.json` with `--out-dtype fp32`).
Official config 60d8d707: H=4096, vocab 129280, 64 indexer heads; other
attention, shared-expert and MTP linears are block-FP8 and routed experts MXFP4
(not Linear rows). The router GEMM (256x4096, FP32 out) is fused into the
dsv4-moe router kernel and not covered here.

Market (same call in vLLM 0.31.0 and SGLang 72730d6 on sm120/sm121, cuBLAS):
`lm_head` F.linear (vLLM) / torch.matmul (SGLang), BF16 out; indexer
`weights_proj` F.linear; compressor `wkv_gate` (fused wkv+wgate) and the indexer
compressor `torch.mm(x, w.t(), out_dtype=torch.float32)`. cuBLAS is timed with
torch's default flags. A row ships CuTe only when its configuration beats
cuBLAS by > max(1.03, 1+ε) in the cold, engine (alternating cold/hot weights)
and hot regimes at both bucket edges, from a run without FAIL rows; every other
row is cuBLAS (×1.000 by construction). Times and SOL are cold (rotating
weights: a decode step streams every weight once). SOL uses the round-3
measured DRAM read ceilings 1681.1 / 1533.1 / 244.6 GB/s (5090 / PRO 6000 /
GB10). GB10 runs are pinned to the X925 cores. "ours µs" is the dispatch measured during
acceptance (the CuTe candidate where one was tabled, else the cuBLAS fallback).
Retries resolve with the shared evidence resolver; a correctness FAIL would show
as FAIL and its timings would be left out of the per-step totals. These logs
contain none.

Below-90%-SOL rows: weights_proj (0.5 MiB) and the FP32 compressors on the
RTX cards (4-16 MiB) are launch/latency bound (one or two kernels of 3-14 µs).
On GB10 the `reg` variant's 8-row x 64-byte weight loads cap DRAM reads at
~207 GB/s (85%), and its `ld.global.nc.L1::no_allocate` weight lines are not kept
in GB10's L2, so a recurring weight never hits there (engine time = cold time).
SM6 adds the `wide` variant (4 rows x 128 bytes per warp load, MMA fragments
rebuilt with one xor-4 shuffle, plain L2-allocating loads). The GB10 FP32
compressors ship it at 1024 M=1 and M=9-16, 2048 M=1 and 512 M=2-8 (cold
1.10-1.38x, engine 1.20-2.36x, hot 1.52-2.26x). They stay on cuBLAS at 2048 M>=2
(a tie, both at ~90% SOL), 1024 M=2-8 (1.02x cold at M=2-5), 512 M=9-16 (gain
inside epsilon) and 512 M=1 (cuBLAS GEMV). Disclosed, not chased: GB10 lm_head
M>=2 stays on cuBLAS at 95-96% of the 244.6 GB/s ceiling (4.51 ms vs 4.33 ms);
no CuTe variant clears 1.03x there. FP32 2048x4096 on PRO 6000 loses only in the
engine regime and stays on cuBLAS. GB10 rows with a bracketed bootstrap 95% CI
of the paired speedup are SM6 60-pair records (every shipped GB10 CuTe row and
the compressors); the other GB10 rows (cuBLAS fallbacks: lm_head M>=2,
weights_proj M=1/9/16) are the earlier 20-pair records. A GB10 CuTe row needs
CI_low > 1 in the cold, engine and hot records at both bucket edges.
